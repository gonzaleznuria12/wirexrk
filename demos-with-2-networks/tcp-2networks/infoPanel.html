Controls & Actions

  • Use the three panel buttons to switch views, play/pause, or reset the animation. Adjust speed with the slider below.
  • Move using the joystick (controller) or arrow keys (desktop).
  • Aim the laser at a message header or device (PC, router, switch) and pull the trigger to toggle info. On desktop, use the mouse instead.
